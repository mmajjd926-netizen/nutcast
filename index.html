            Nutzaps (NIP-61)

            Some players (nodesignal's cashu-player, Nutcast itself) send ecash locked to a key you publish in a kind 10019 event, as public kind 9321 events. Publish yours here: the mints are the ones above (or this wallet's mint). If you already have a NIP-60 wallet on Nostr (kind 17375), its key is used, so that wallet and Nutcast redeem the same nutzaps; otherwise a lock key is made here, apart from your Nostr key, and saved as your NIP-60 wallet. "Check for payments" then also redeems nutzaps, and marks each one redeemed (kind 7376) so your other wallets skip it.
